Galdralag hardware key registry

This is not a general-purpose OpenPGP keyserver. Fulla is a closed, project-local registry for public keys belonging to owners and maintainers of Galdralag hardware. It does not gossip with SKS, HKP, or keys.openpgp.org; it does not accept anonymous uploads.

Anyone landing here should understand immediately:

Fulla is a private key registry for owners of Galdralag hardware only. It is not a general-purpose OpenPGP keyserver. If you are looking for someone's public key and they do not own Galdralag hardware, this is the wrong place. Keys here were generated on Galdralag hardware tokens by members of the Galdralag amateur-radio community.

Keys must include at least one community identifier alongside a validated mailbox (such as a callsign, DMR ID, or radio-affiliation hint). Algorithms are constrained to match what Galdralag tokens generate; uploads that diverge from that profile are refused.

Use the firmware galdra keyserver CLI to push or fetch when your configuration points at this host.
